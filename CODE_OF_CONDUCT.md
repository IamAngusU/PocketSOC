# Code of conduct

We want PocketSOC to be a technically rigorous and welcoming defensive-security project.

Be respectful, critique ideas with evidence, protect other people's privacy, and assume that contributors have different levels of security experience. Harassment, doxxing, publication of private traffic or credentials, discriminatory language, and attempts to redirect the project toward unauthorized access are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and community participation that violate these expectations. Sensitive conduct reports should use the repository's private maintainer contact or private vulnerability-reporting channel rather than a public issue.
